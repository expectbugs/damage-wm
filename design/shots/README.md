# Rendered shell surfaces

Everything here is generated by `design/render_shots.py` at **true 1× 640×480**, quantised to
4bpp and priced through the firmware's own RLE, except the files listed below. Regenerate after
any design change; `tools/lint.py` checks the results against the ink budgets in `DESIGN.md`
§4.2 (BUD005/006) and against that file's own stated measurements (BUD007).

Never render at 2×. A 2× view flatters delicate type and misled the typeface evaluation for
several passes — at true scale, stroke weight decides legibility and letterform character is
secondary.

Six older renders are kept but no longer produced by the script: `cmp-288-band-occluded.png`,
`cmp-480-rail.png`, `cmp-480-ribbon-occluded.png` (the 288-band / icon-rail / ribbon
comparison, dropped from the script 2026-08-18 once the decision landed — `DESIGN.md` §2.2b
still cites the ribbon one), `battery-granularity.png` (the three battery encodings compared in
`DESIGN.md` §4.1), `main-font-compare.png` (the typeface evaluation, cited in `DESIGN.md` §Type)
and `mixed-fonts-pair.png` (from the same evaluation; cited nowhere). They are reference
material for the sections that cite them, not surfaces the linter checks.

## Not ours
